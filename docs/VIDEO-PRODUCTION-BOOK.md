# CareMed Academy · Filming book and detailed sequence outlines

Each script is a **production-ready outline**, not an already shot or approved full-length video. Use the actual converted Toyota Sienna with a verified professional instructor. Any demonstration of wheelchair handling must match the conversion and restraint manuals. Film using a stationary safety spotter and a trained simulation participant.

## Film 01 | Safe arrival and pre-trip inspection (~8 min)

**00:00–01:00** Opening: "What a passenger sees as routine transportation can require careful decisions from the driver. Before boarding, we evaluate the vehicle, the equipment, and the environment." Show wide establishing shot and driver walking around van. On-screen label: "Training draft · confirm equipment manuals".

**01:00–02:30** Real equipment inventory. Film actual ramp stow location and identification labels; driver reads manufacturer/model/serial, checks inspection records. Narration placeholder: "The ramp installed in this van governs our procedures. Never substitute generic instructions." Identify no-go conditions based on manual.

**02:30–04:15** Approaching the pickup. Demonstrate traffic isolation, pedestrian clearance, stable level landing, door/rear-gate clearance, weather and lighting assessment. Freeze-frame decision points: blocked curb, sloped driveway, icy surface, and standing water. If unsafe, stop and escalate.

**04:15–05:45** Consent and communication. Speak directly to passenger, ask how they want assistance, discuss service boundary. Never touch mobility equipment without permission absent an immediate safety need.

**05:45–07:15** Demonstrate the actual manufacturer-prescribed pre-use checks using close-up views and manual references, deliberately omit any actions not verified in actual manufacturer documentation.

**07:15–08:00** Quiz and handoff to supervised practical demonstration. Acknowledge hands-on evaluation as mandatory.

## Film 02 | Rear-ramp boarding and unloading (~12 min)

**00:00–01:30** Vehicle model/ramp identifier, capacity label and contraindications, manual citations on screen.

**01:30–03:30** Environment assessment and positioning. Uninterrupted wide shot and third-person spoken risk check.

**03:30–06:30** Approved boarding demo, one continuous camera angle, then second close-up angle: demonstrate only actions approved by manufacturer/authorized trainer. Instructor identifies wheelchair type and any needed spotter. Include patient consent before assistance. Do not script generic backing or forward orientation without manual confirmation.

**06:30–09:00** Powered wheelchair variation; show communication and stop/ask-for-help choices rather than improvisation.

**09:00–10:45** Unloading with full environment re-check and correct ramp procedure for the actual model.

**10:45–12:00** Stop-work examples: blocked ramp, slope, ice, malfunction. Quiz with real footage. Avoid filming real uncontrolled rollaways.

## Film 03 | Securement and occupant protection (~12 min)

**00:00–01:45** Two independent systems: wheelchair tie-down and occupant restraint. Compare with color-coded visual overlays.

**01:45–03:00** Verify actual WTORS brand, model, markings and approved securement locations.

**03:00–07:00** Filmed step-by-step approved wheelchair restraint application with uninterrupted wide shot, then closeups, exact maker manual annotations.

**07:00–09:30** Occupant lap/shoulder restraint demonstration; pay special attention to correctly approved belt paths and comfort, based on system manual.

**09:30–10:45** Scooter/tilt-in-space variations and no-improvisation escalation.

**10:45–12:00** Instructor-led competency checkpoints, each with observed critical pass criteria.

## Film 04 | Passenger-first assistance (~8 min)

Script scenarios: greeting directly; visually impaired passenger approaches ramp; deaf passenger prefers text; passenger with dementia becomes disoriented; service animal accompanies passenger; unexpected requested physical transfer. For each, include a paused decision with safe path and respectful wording. Do not imply every passenger with a particular condition needs the same assistance.

## Film 05 | Emergencies and equipment defects (~10 min)

Storyboard: medical distress -> safe stop -> 911 -> dispatch -> follow scope. Ramp fails with wheelchair near or on it -> stabilize scene -> only manufacturer-approved recovery procedures and trained personnel -> coordinate emergency help. Collision or fire -> follow trained evacuation plan, never improvise unsafe lift. Include post-incident documentation and internal notification.

## Film 06 | Illinois winter driving and loading (~8 min)

Storyboard: forecast planning, safe tire/vehicle check, longer stopping distance, arrival on snow, icy ramp landing/no-go, alternative safe pickup site via dispatch, cold weather communication. Safety action: stop when environment is unsafe.

## Film 07 | Practical verification (~6 min)

Instructor shows applicant a blank checklist, confirms ID and vehicle, observes each criterion in real time, records a failure without intimidation, documents remediation and retest, and explains independent secondary admin review. Neither online passing scores nor a checkbox replaces observed competency.

## Video publishing specification

- Source master: 4K or 1080p, 16:9 at 24/30 fps, clean 48kHz spoken audio; distribution H.264 MP4 and ideally adaptive streaming for scale.
- Captions: professionally corrected WebVTT (`.vtt`), not auto-caption draft; provide transcript as accessible HTML.
- Illustrations: original graphics labeled "schematic" unless actual equipment verified; minimum 16px captions on mobile.
- Each video includes immutable version, applicability (vehicle/ramp/WTORS), approval owner, review date and link to actual approved manufacturer manual.
- Publish behind authentication with permission-controlled media delivery for employees; avoid placing confidential training records in a public static directory.
- Obtain written image/person/video releases and vendor reuse permission.
