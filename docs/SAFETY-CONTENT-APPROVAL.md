## Equipment evidence before video production

For EACH vehicle (not just each vehicle make), fleet management must record:
- Year, VIN, vehicle capacity, conversion manufacturer and conversion model/serial.
- Ramp model/serial, manual vs powered, maximum capacity, inspection and fault-recovery instructions from the corresponding OEM/converter manual.
- Wheelchair tie-down and occupant restraint (WTORS) brand/model, fitted seatbelt anchors, maker manual, inspection/maintenance intervals.
- Approved wheelchair categories, allowable vehicle configurations, known limits and required special equipment.
- Photo/video release from CareMed, riders and any depicted actors; confidentiality/consent documentation.

**Do not assume the Toyota Sienna uses BraunAbility or VMI:** both produce different conversion configurations. Use the actual plates and manuals.

## Required video production set (scripts drafted in `VIDEO-PRODUCTION-BOOK.md`)

1. **Pre-trip and safe arrival**: ~8 minutes; instructor demonstration, safe stopping, traffic/pedestrian assessment, winter surface risks; film actual vehicle.
2. **Ramp boarding and unloading**: ~12 minutes; uninterrupted real-time operator demonstration of the exact installed rear-entry ramp, plus close-ups and alternate powered wheelchair example.
3. **Wheelchair and occupant restraint**: ~12 minutes; clear interior camera positions, manufacturer-approved securement points, belt routing and final review, no generic device-specific claims.
4. **Passenger dignity, communication and handoff**: ~8 minutes; scripted passenger actors, service boundary examples, hearing/vision/cognitive accommodations.
5. **Emergency response and equipment failures**: ~10 minutes; simulated situations under qualified supervision, 911/dispatch protocols, manufacturer's approved failure procedures.
6. **Illinois winter operations**: ~8 minutes; real outdoor surface assessment and no-go decisions.
7. **Assessment orientation**: ~6 minutes; instructor explaining requirements, unsafe actions, remediation, evidence and dual review.

All duration estimates are editorial targets, not finished footage. The existing 12-second MP4 clips are **conceptual placeholders** and are not approved equipment demonstrations.

## Media approval workflow (required outside this pilot)

1. Training author drafts script and a line-by-line source matrix.
2. Qualified wheelchair-transportation instructor reviews safety and passenger interaction.
3. The exact equipment manufacturer or authorized trainer confirms each equipment action **in writing**; log reviewer, date, document/version and scope of approval.
4. Illinois compliance counsel/transportation compliance lead verifies applicability and claims, including HFS training and Chicago/county requirements.
5. Accessibility QA adds accurate captions, transcripts, audio description where needed, keyboard-usable interactions, and contrast checks.
6. CareMed safety director signs internal release; publish with version, approval artifact, expiration/review date, equipment applicability and content checksum.
7. Revoke outdated versions and require retraining where safety procedures change.

No approval evidence is included in this deliverable. The pilot's internal "approved" flag is an editorial flag only, never manufacturer or HFS approval.
